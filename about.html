    The team

    Meet Nicola and Leanne

    Nicola and Leanne at Nicola’s Barbershop on Station Road, Deganwy — expert grooming and a warm welcome (Maisie included).

    Nicola and Leanne

    With over 20 years of combined experience, Nicola and Leanne bring a wealth of knowledge and skill to every cut. We pride ourselves on our attention to detail, ensuring each client leaves our shop feeling their best.

    Nicola

    Salon Owner

    Hi, I’m Nicola. I opened Nicola’s Barbers in 2017, after working in the local area for three years before that. I pride myself on the small details. I’m happy to chat, or if you’re the silent type, I’m also comfortable in the silence. My interests are travelling, if you need a conversation starter.

    Leanne

    Barber

    Hi, I’m Leanne. I’m local to the area as well and I’ve been cutting hair since 2014. I’m the chatty one in the salon! I’m very good with the kids, being a mum of one myself. My hobbies are football, in which I represent a local women’s team, and track days in mine and my partner’s Civic Type R.

    Adding to the friendly vibe of our shop is Maisie, our lovable canine companion. If you’re not a dog lover, then our barbershop is not the best fit for you.
