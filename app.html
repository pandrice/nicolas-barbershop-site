    The app

    Get the Nicola’s app

    Put the shop on your phone’s home screen. Book, call and find us in one tap. It’s free, takes about ten seconds and there’s nothing to download from an app store.

    You’ve already got the app. Nice one.

    iPhone or iPad

    You need to use Safari for this (it won’t work from inside Instagram or Facebook’s browser).

    1. Open nicolasbarbershop.co.uk in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    4. Tap Add in the top right corner.

    Android

    Use Chrome (Samsung Internet works too).

    1. Open nicolasbarbershop.co.uk in Chrome.
    2. Tap the three dots menu in the top right corner.
    3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
    4. Tap Install to confirm.

    It opens full screen, just like an app. Booking is always the live Nearcut calendar, so you’ll see the real times. Prefer not to? The website works exactly the same in your browser.
