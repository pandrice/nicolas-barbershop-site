    Booking

    Policy

    House rules for booking, cancellations and payments. Phone: 07821 865044.

    Booking

    Please only book if you agree to these terms. Book a time online via Nearcut, or call 07821 865044.

    Cancellations & no-shows

    Please give at least 12 hours’ notice. Less than that, or a no-show, means the full appointment fee is due. If that fee isn’t paid, the account is blocked for future bookings.

    How to cancel or reschedule

    • Online through your Nearcut account, if it’s more than 12 hours before the appointment, or
    • Phone 07821 865044.

    Payments

    Card (including Amex) or cash.

    Maisie (shop dog)

    Maisie is usually in the shop. If you’re not a dog person, we’re not the best fit.

    Privacy & booking data

    While booking runs on Nearcut, customer account and booking data is handled under Nearcut’s systems and policies as well as ours. When we move booking off Nearcut later, we’ll update this page.
