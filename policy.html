Booking

Cancellations & no-shows

Please give at least 12 hours’ notice. Less than that, or a no-show, means the full appointment fee is due. If that fee isn’t paid, the account is blocked for future bookings.

How to cancel or reschedule

  • Online through your Nearcut account, if it’s more than 12 hours before the appointment, or
  • Phone 07821 865044.

Payments
